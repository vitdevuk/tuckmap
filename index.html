The map · live data

Pick a town.
See who is cooking.

These are real places. The names, addresses and hygiene ratings come live from the Food Standards Agency register, the official list of food businesses in the UK.

Tap a dot on the map or a name in the list to open that kitchen.

The price

The kitchen's price,
plus £1.50 a dish.

The restaurant sets its menu price and keeps it. We add one flat £1.50 per dish for running the map, the app and the order. You see both numbers before you pay.

The app

A food guide
in your pocket.

One screen, every kitchen around you. Wherever you tap, the menu opens: what they cook, what it costs, how to order.

  • Works in every UK town

    The map is built on the national register, so a new town needs no setup.

  • Tap, read, order

    No searching through ten websites. The menu is one tap from the map.

  • Local kitchens first

    Independent takeaways sit on the same map as the big names.
